## 1. Market Overview

The clipping industry is the economy of freelance editors ("clippers") who cut long-form content (podcasts, streams, interviews, YouTube videos) into short-form vertical clips (15–60s) for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels. Clippers get paid per 1,000 views (CPM model).

4. **Arabic content is underserved by tools** — only 23% of AI tools support Arabic properly
5. **Clipping agencies have built the demand** — Arabic Clipping has 50K+ clippers but no self-serve tooling

## 4. Clipper Personas (MENA)

### Persona 1: Independent Clipper

- **Who:** Freelancer, often 18–30, multilingual (Arabic + English)
- **Where:** Joins campaigns on Arabic Clipping (Whop), Clipping.net, or Discord servers
- **Workflow:** Picks a campaign → downloads source video → finds moments → cuts clips → posts to own TikTok/Reels/Shorts → gets paid per view
- **Current tools:** CapCut (manual), OpusClip (AI guessing), screen recording
- **Pain point:** Spends 30–60 min per clip finding the right moment. AI tools guess wrong often. Needs to produce 5–15 clips per source video to earn well.
- **Willingness to pay:** $15–25/mo for a tool that saves time and produces better clips

### Persona 2: Arabic Content Creator

- **Who:** YouTuber/podcaster with long-form Arabic content (20K–200K subscribers)
- **Where:** YouTube, wants to repurpose into shorts
- **Workflow:** Records long-form → wants to clip best moments → currently watches full video manually or uses AI tools that don't understand Arabic
- **Current tools:** Manual review, OpusClip (poor Arabic support)
- **Pain point:** AI tools produce bad Arabic captions. No tool uses heatmap data on their own channel's audience retention.
- **Willingness to pay:** $15–25/mo

### Persona 3: Clipping Agency Team Lead

- **Who:** Runs a small clipping operation (5–20 clippers) in MENA
- **Where:** Manages campaigns for MENA brands
- **Workflow:** Assigns source videos to clippers → clippers cut and post → tracks performance
- **Current tools:** Spreadheets, Discord, manual coordination
- **Pain point:** No standardized tooling for their clipper network. Each clipper uses different tools, different quality.
- **Willingness to pay:** $40–60/mo for team accounts
